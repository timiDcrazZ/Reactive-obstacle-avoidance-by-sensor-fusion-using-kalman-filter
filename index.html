System summary
Sensor behaviour, key metrics, and material response overview.
For reactive obstacle avoidance, overestimation is the critical failure — the drone believes it is farther from an obstacle than it actually is and keeps flying, leading to collision. Underestimation causes the drone to stop early — conservative but safe.

Material-by-material insights
Key observations for each material relevant to drone obstacle avoidance.
Both sensors reliable
Wood
ToF reads slightly low at 25 cm (23.5 vs 25). Ultrasonic fused stays within ±0.3 cm across all distances. Ideal baseline calibration surface. Low disagreement — base weights sufficient.
Ultrasonic excellent
Metal / Aluminum
ToF under-reads slightly — IR specular reflection at angles bounces away from sensor. Ultrasonic handles metal well — sound reflects cleanly. Both sensors underestimate → safe for obstacle avoidance.
ToF struggles at 75 cm
Glass
ToF reads 70.3 cm at 75 cm (4.7 cm error) — IR partially passes through transparent surfaces. Disagreement exceeds 3 cm → safety override activates → shorter reading used → safe outcome.
Most consistent overall
Concrete / Wall
Best results for both sensors across all distances. Large flat diffuse surface reflects both IR and ultrasonic perfectly. Ideal calibration reference. Both sensors agree within 0.5 cm.
ToF varies with colour
Coloured plastic
Dark plastics absorb more IR — ToF error increases. Ultrasonic fused remains accurate. Disagreement moderate but within threshold at most distances. Base weights handle this well.
Both sensors affected
Fabric
Most challenging material for both sensors. Fabric absorbs sound → ultrasonic overestimates by ~1.2 cm at 75 cm (dangerous). ToF inconsistent due to IR scattering. Highest noise. Override rule most critical here.
